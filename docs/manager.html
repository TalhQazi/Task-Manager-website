Manager Panel Overview

The Manager Panel is designed for operational leadership, focusing on team productivity, task execution, and resource coordination.

Core Responsibilities

📈 Operational Dashboard

  • Monitor department-specific pending and overdue tasks.
  • Track real-time employee clock-in status.
  • Review recent system activity and event logs.

✅ Task & Project Management

  • Create, assign, and monitor team tasks with strict deadlines.
  • Set priorities and provide detailed execution guidelines.
  • Review End-of-Day (EOD) reports to track incremental progress.

📅 Team Scheduling

  • Manage and adjust shift schedules for team members.
  • Monitor shift compliance and attendance.
  • Process and review employee leave requests.

🚗 Resource Allocation

  • View available company vehicles and appliances.
  • Assign resources to specific employees or projects.
  • Ensure efficient utilization of operational assets.
